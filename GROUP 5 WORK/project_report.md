# Hostel Room Booking & Fees Management System Report

### General Purpose

To replace the slow, error-prone paper ledger used by the university hostel warden with an automated, reliable Python-based management system.

### Goals

- **Room Allocation:** Automate student registration and room assignment across three hostel blocks while preventing over-allocation.
- **Financial Tracking:** Record full or partial fee payments and maintain updated student balances.
- **Search & Reporting:** Enable quick student lookup by name or registration number, generate block occupancy overviews, and filter fee defaulters.
- **Persistence & Usability:** Persist records to file with graceful error handling and provide an intuitive, validated menu-driven interface for non-technical wardens.

### Expected Outcomes

- **Operational Efficiency:** Eliminates manual record-keeping errors and booking conflicts.
- **Financial Oversight:** Delivers instant, accurate visibility into fee arrears and defaulter lists.
- **Data Integrity:** Ensures records survive application restarts with robust handling for missing or corrupted files.
- **User-Friendly Operation:** Empowers hostel wardens to easily navigate and manage hostel operations without technical background.
